Kestrel 2.3 = Kestrel 2.2 (obsolete options with wrong defaults removed) plus the fix of an int32 overflow in the layer-2 sums of the network: the sums over the 1,536 products are now accumulated in float32 (AVX2 and plain code, same order); the same network and search.
The bug (all versions 2.0 - 2.2): a strongly negative layer-2 neuron can have an exact sum below -2^31; the 32-bit sum wrapped around and the neuron was read as fully on instead of off. Found by UBSan on Linux (plain code path, src/kestrel_2_0.cpp:354 of 2.2: signed integer overflow -2146184439 + -7141898).
Counted in the depth-12 bench with 64-bit exact sums (plain code path): 797,651 evaluations, 12,762,416 neuron sums, 9 exact sums outside int32 (bucket 0 = at most 5 pieces, neurons 2 and 4: -2.26e9 .. -2.50e9).
After the fix: bench 1,051,844 nodes on the x86-64-v3, x86-64-v2 and generic builds (unchanged); bench time median of five runs 770 ms (2.2: 771 ms).
Identity check against 2.2 (default settings, one thread, go nodes 300000, 18 positions): identical in 16 of 18; the two others are endgames (a rook ending: same best move, score and pv, 194,602 instead of 216,294 nodes to depth 15; a pawn ending: same best move, score -584 instead of -544).
Match: fastchess, 5+0.1, 6 games at a time, the same openings for both sides: 2.3 against 2.2: Elo +6.95 +/- 20.86 (W 117, L 109, D 174; 400 games, all terminations normal).
Checks: audits 83 of 83 and 31 of 31; options test 0 differences; 27-check sanitizer verification (AVX2 build, GCC and clang ASan/UBSan, also 4 threads) 27 of 27, 689 s (logs/v2_3_verify_log.txt).
